    Missable trophies — watch out

    “Missable” here means the trophy is tied to one attempt, one moment, or one set of choices, so failing the condition means doing the attempt again. Nothing here is confirmed missable by the publisher — the game is unreleased — so each entry says what the description proves and what is only a precaution. Cheap insurance for the whole list: keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission.
